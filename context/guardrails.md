# Guardrails Policy – Rya Rightsizing

## 1. Claims

- All marketing content must be accurate and consistent with Rya Rightsizing's confirmed services and Brand Book.
- Do not invent statistics, customer testimonials, business results, certifications, or professional credentials.
- Do not promise guaranteed outcomes, stress-free moves, or specific results.
- Describe Rya's role accurately. Rya provides personalized rightsizing and move management support and coordinates third-party service providers when needed.
- Do not claim that Rya directly provides transportation or full-service packing.
- Do not publish pricing, discounts, or service guarantees without Lisa's approval.
- Use "rightsizing" as the primary term and "downsizing" as a secondary search term.

## 2. Privacy

- Do not publish real client names, addresses, photographs, personal stories, or identifying information without written permission.
- Obtain appropriate consent before using before-and-after photographs or testimonials.
- Do not upload confidential client information into AI tools without authorization.
- Use fictional or composite personas for marketing research.
- Respect the privacy of older adults and their families throughout the marketing process.

## 3. AI Disclosure

- AI may support research, content drafting, editing, and marketing planning.
- All AI-generated content must be reviewed by a team member before publication.
- AI-generated information must be checked against reliable sources and the approved client context.
- Clearly disclose AI-generated or significantly AI-altered images, testimonials, or representations when their use could mislead the audience.
- Do not present AI-generated personas, quotations, or scenarios as real customer experiences.
- Lisa must approve client-facing materials before publication.

## 4. Audience Sensitivity

- Use a warm, compassionate, reassuring, and non-judgmental tone.
- Respect older adults' independence, dignity, belongings, and personal decisions.
- Avoid stereotypes about aging, health, abilities, or family relationships.
- Do not use fear, guilt, pressure, or urgency to encourage customers to book services.
- Avoid negative expressions such as "junk" or "throw away" when referring to personal belongings.
- Recognize that adult children may coordinate services while older adults remain involved in decisions.
- Use clear, accessible, and respectful language.
- Emphasize personalized support, peace of mind, and the client's ability to move at their own pace.

## Review and Approval

- All marketing materials must follow the Brand Book, confirmed service offer, and this guardrails policy.
- Any unverified information must be marked as "Assumption – validate with client."
- Client-facing content requires human review and Lisa's approval before publication.
- This policy remains a draft until reviewed and approved by the team.

Source: Rya Rightsizing Brand Book, Team Marketing Plan (October 22, 2026), and Research-Personas Station Guidelines.
