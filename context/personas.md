# Rya Rightsizing - Target Personas

## Persona 1: The Sandwich Generation (Primary Audience)

- Age: 45-65
- Profile: Working adults balancing careers, children, and aging parents.
- Location: North Vancouver, West Vancouver, and the Sea-to-Sky corridor.
- Needs: Help coordinating a parent's downsizing and home transition.
- Pain points: Limited time, family coordination challenges, emotional stress, and concerns about costs.
- Motivations: Convenience, peace of mind, transparent pricing, and trustworthy support.
- Key message: Rya Rightsizing provides compassionate, personalized coordination that helps families navigate home transitions with less stress.
- Relevant channels: Local search, educational content, and real estate referrals.

## Persona 2: Active Seniors (Secondary Audience)

- Age: 65+
- Profile: Older adults considering downsizing or reorganizing their homes while maintaining independence.
- Location: North Vancouver, West Vancouver, and the Sea-to-Sky corridor.
- Needs: Patient assistance with decluttering, organizing belongings, and coordinating a move.
- Pain points: Emotional attachment to belongings and concerns about losing control of decisions.
- Motivations: Independence, dignity, comfort, and respectful support.
- Key message: Rya Rightsizing supports clients at their own pace while respecting their choices and belongings.
- Relevant channels: Word-of-mouth and community networks.

## Referral Audience: Real Estate Professionals

- Profile: Local real estate agents assisting families with property transitions.
- Role: Potential referral partners rather than primary end clients.
- Needs: Clear information about Rya's services and referral process.
- Relevant materials: Digital referral cards, listing flyers, and introductory emails.

## Brand Communication Guidelines

- Use a warm, compassionate, reassuring, and non-judgmental voice.
- Respect clients' autonomy and decisions.
- Emphasize dignity, peace of mind, and thoughtful organization.
- Avoid urgency, pressure, and language such as 'junk' or 'throw away'.
- Do not promise transportation or full-service packing; Rya coordinates third-party providers.
