# Rya Rightsizing: Brand Positioning

*Context file for the Rya Factory. Source of truth is the brand book; where this file and the brand book differ, the brand book wins. Source key: (L) Lisa's files · (W) website · (R) recommendation.*

## 1. Market definition and category

- **Category:** Compassionate senior move management: rightsizing, downsizing, decluttering and moving support for older adults and their families.
- **Service area:** North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor (Squamish, Whistler and between). Office in Squamish. North & West Vancouver is a key target market. (W)
- **Core distinction:** Rya practises *rightsizing*: finding the right balance between a person's space, their belongings and the life they're living now. It is not about having less. Downsizing can feel like loss or pressure; rightsizing is making intentional room for what matters.
- **Who we are not:** a moving company or a junk-removal company. Rya treats a lifetime of belongings as a story to honour, not a load to clear.
- **Primary tagline:** Compassionate rightsizing, downsizing + move services for seniors.
- **Secondary brand line:** Guided by heart. Grounded in compassion.

## 2. Target audience

**Primary buyer: the adult child.** Busy, often living far away. Feels guilty, stretched and worried. Wants a trustworthy local person and clear communication. Often the one who finds Rya and books the consultation.

**Person at the centre: the senior.** Staying in place, moving to a smaller home, or moving to senior living. May feel overwhelmed or attached to belongings, may be grieving, and may have limited mobility. Wants respect, their own pace, and to keep what matters.

**Secondary audiences:**
- Estate executors and grieving families clearing a loved one's home
- Individuals decluttering, including storage units and very full homes (always non-judgmental)
- Referral partners: realtors, estate and elder-law lawyers, senior living communities, movers, non-profits

**What they all want:** a calm, reassuring professional, not a high-pressure sales pitch.

**Test for any message:** Would this help a worried adult child, or their parent, feel calmer and more capable?

## 3. Positioning statement

For families in North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor helping an aging parent rightsize, downsize or move, Rya Rightsizing is the compassionate, local move specialist that handles both the logistics and the emotions, so every family member feels supported, informed and at peace, even from a distance. Unlike a moving or junk-removal company, Rya treats a lifetime of belongings as a story to honour, not a load to clear. (Brand book, section 2, R)

## 4. Unique value proposition

Rya Rightsizing offers hands-on, empathetic organization and move management for seniors and their families. Overwhelming decisions become manageable, structured steps, with one dedicated family liaison as the single point of contact. Families keep what matters, give the rest a good next home, and gain peace of mind.

## 5. Brand positioning pillars

1. **Calm and unhurried.** No artificial urgency, no fear-based selling. Your pace, your plan.
2. **Dignity-first.** Belongings carry memories and meaning. Honour the stories behind them. Dignity is non-negotiable.
3. **Tangible relief.** Real, visible results (sorting, donations, packing, movers) while holding the client through hard decisions.
4. **Connected family.** One dedicated family liaison keeps everyone informed, including adult children far away.
5. **Local and community-minded.** Squamish-based, rooted in the Sea to Sky, with items directed to local non-profits and landfill diversion where possible.

## 9. Messaging rules (from the brand book)

**Words and phrases**
- Lead with **"rightsizing"** as the primary word for what Rya does. Use **"downsizing"** as a secondary term because it's what people search for: in page titles, headings, meta descriptions, URLs, hashtags and alongside rightsizing ("rightsizing and downsizing"). Where there's room, explain: "We call it rightsizing, because it's not about having less."
- Say **"complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual"**, never "free consultation."
- Say **"three-hour blocks of time or more"**, never "sessions" or "minimum." Never say "no long-term contract" or "all-or-nothing."
- Anchor geography to **North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor**. Never "Metro Vancouver." Use #NorthAndWestVancouver on social.
- Naming: "Rya Rightsizing" in full on first reference, "Rya" after. Never "RYA," "Rya's Rightsizing" or "Rya Right Sizing." 
- Use "and" in body copy; "+" only in taglines and short headers.

**Avoid:** junk (about belongings), purge, get rid of, elderly, "old folks," hoarder as a label, "stuff" in formal copy, cheap, fast/quick, cluttered mess, "before it's too late," urgency CTAs.

**Primary CTA:** Book your complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual.

## 10. Voice and personality (summary)

A calm, capable friend who shows up with a plan, a kind word and packing tape. Compassionate, reassuring, respectful, clear and practical, hopeful. "We" for Rya, "you" for the reader. Canadian spelling, grade 7–8 reading level, short sentences, exclamation marks rarely, no emoji in service copy and sparing on social. Full tone-by-situation table is in the brand book, section 7.

## 11. Claims guardrails for the marketing plan

- Don't invent prices, services, credentials, testimonials, statistics or results.
- Pricing on the website: from $80/hr per specialist, in three-hour blocks of time or more (from $240). The needs assessment is $80, which conflicts with the website FAQ wording. 
- Seniors are shown capable and at ease, never frail or clinical.
- The older woman in the session and B-roll photos is a client.
