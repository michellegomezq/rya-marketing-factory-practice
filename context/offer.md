
# Offer – Rya Rightsizing

## Services

Rya Rightsizing provides personalized rightsizing, downsizing, home organization, and move management support for older adults and their families.

Services include:

- Assistance with sorting and organizing belongings.
- Support with decluttering and preparing for a home transition.
- Personalized planning and coordination of moving activities.
- Coordination with third-party service providers when needed.
- Compassionate guidance throughout the rightsizing process.

Rya Rightsizing coordinates moving-related services but does not directly provide transportation or full-service packing.

## Target Audience

- Older adults who are planning to rightsize or reorganize their homes.
- Adult children helping their parents navigate a home transition.
- Families seeking personalized support during the moving process.

## Service Area

- North Vancouver
- West Vancouver
- Sea-to-Sky corridor

## Consultation

Rya Rightsizing offers a complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual.

## Pricing

Specific service prices have not been confirmed for public use. No pricing claims should be published without client approval.

## Brand Approach

Rya Rightsizing emphasizes dignity, independence, compassion, and respect for clients' belongings and decisions.

The goal is to make home transitions more manageable while allowing clients to move at their own pace.
