## 1. Executive summary
The objective of this plan is to improve visibility, build trust and bring more clients to Rya Rightsizing in North Vancouver, West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor. Rya Rightsizing offers personalized rightsizing, downsizing and move-management support for older adults and their families. The primary buyer is the adult child helping an aging parent. The person at the centre is the older adult going through the transition.

Within 12 weeks, the goal is 12 qualified leads, 6 introductory meetings and 2 paying clients, on a marketing budget of $500 per month. The work focuses on three strategies: building local trust and authority, building referral relationships with real estate professionals, and making Rya easier to find through local SEO and AI answer tools (GEO).

A 12-week content calendar puts the plan into action through educational social posts, guide articles, referral material and website content. AI tools help draft and adapt content. A team member reviews every asset, and Lisa approves anything client-facing. Results are reviewed in Weeks 4, 8 and 12.

## 4. Positioning and messaging

**Positioning: "The Guiding Light."** (P) Rya Rightsizing is a compassionate, local senior move-management and rightsizing service for families in North and West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor. It is not a rushed commercial moving company or a junk hauler. It is a calm, unhurried coordinator who handles both the logistics and the emotions. The brand puts client autonomy, dignity and calm pacing ahead of corporate efficiency. It aims to be the service where everyone feels comfortable reaching out. The name honours Lisa's friend Raija, whose name means "guiding light" (W).

**Differentiators**
- Personalized coordination
- One dedicated family liaison
- Emotional support
- Respect for belongings and decisions
- Local roots and community connection

**Content pillars** (exact names from the content calendar; the calendar reuses these)
Brand introduction · Education & reassurance · Service clarity · Trust & objections · Real estate partner education · Practical partner resource · Community connection · Referral pathway · Search-led education · FAQ & AI-search readiness · Paid amplification test · Results and next steps

**Messaging guardrails** (P, aligned with the brand book)
- Use respectful language. Replace "junk," "throw away" and "clean out" with "belongings," "donate" and "thoughtfully organize."
- Preserve client autonomy. Clients make the decisions, at their own pace.
- Focus on comfort, peace of mind and support through change, not loss or pressure.
- Communicate service boundaries and coordination responsibilities accurately. Publish pricing only with Lisa's approval.
- Adapt to each audience. For adult children, stress convenience and reduced stress. For seniors, stress dignity and independence.
- Review all AI-assisted content for accuracy, empathy and brand fit. Never invent claims or promise services Rya doesn't provide.

**Words to use** (CP): rightsizing (primary term), downsizing (secondary or search term), complimentary consultation in-home or virtual, three-hour blocks of time or more, belongings, next chapter, at your pace, dignity, peace of mind, family liaison, older adults, seniors.

**Words to avoid** (CP): junk (for belongings), purge, get rid of, elderly, "old folks," hoarder (as a label), "stuff" in formal copy, cheap, fast or quick, "before it's too late," free consultation, minimum, sessions, no long-term contract, all-or-nothing, Metro Vancouver, urgency CTAs.

**Primary CTA:** Book your complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual.

## 5. Objective and KPIs

**SMART objective:** Rya Rightsizing will generate 12 qualified leads, schedule 6 introductory meetings and acquire 2 paying clients from North Vancouver, West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor between October 26, 2026 and January 17, 2027 (12 weeks), through local SEO, content marketing, and referrals from real estate agents and community connectors.

**Definitions**
- *Qualified lead:* someone in Rya's service area who needs rightsizing or home-transition help and shows interest in Rya's services.
- *Introductory meeting:* a booked complimentary consultation. This is the "Consultation" stage in the tracker.
- *Paying client:* the first paid three-hour block of time has been booked. This is the "Paid Client Start" stage.

**Funnel assumptions** (planning assumptions, not historical data): about 1 qualified lead per week; 50% of leads book a meeting (12 → 6); about 33% of meetings become clients (6 → 2). With a baseline of 4 clients to date, 2 new clients in 12 weeks is a meaningful but modest target for a solo operator.

## 6. Strategies
**S1: Local Trust and Authority** (A: ID). Make Lisa's supportive, empathetic expertise recognizable across North and West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky. *Rationale:* rightsizing is an emotional, trust-led decision, so character and proof matter more than price (team view, P).

**S2: Referral Relationships** (A: ID). Create simple, useful tools for local connectors. This mainly means real estate agents handling downsizing and estate listings, plus community connectors where their policies allow it. *Rationale:* public health authority staff may not refer clients to private businesses [TBC: cite the VCH policy], so private real estate networks and community channels are the main pipeline.

**S3: Discoverability via Local SEO and GEO** (A: ID). Answer real rightsizing and family-transition questions in traditional search engines and in AI answer tools (ChatGPT, Gemini), using local keywords. *Rationale:* adult children look for help online when a parent's move comes up (team assumption, P). Clear, structured FAQs give search engines and AI tools something to cite. Organic results take time to build, so S3 starts in Weeks 2–3 (guide and FAQ) and builds through Weeks 9–11. Most of its effect may land in the next cycle.
